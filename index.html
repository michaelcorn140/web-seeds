About Seeds
Studio.

Seeds Studio is a digital visual studio creating illustration and VTuber work. Many creators have to hunt down separate artists for their model, artwork and stream assets, and the results often don't match. We are one team from Yogyakarta that handles all of it, with a consistent style and a clear process, and a little bit of Jogja in every piece.
